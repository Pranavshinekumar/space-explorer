About Space Explorer

Discover the wonders of our universe.

Space Explorer was created as a project for the Digital Fest at Habitat School, Al Jurf, Ajman, UAE. Our goal is to make learning about space more engaging by exploring planets, stars, black holes, and the mysteries of the universe through an interactive website.
