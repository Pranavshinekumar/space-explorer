About Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It is also the smallest planet in our solar system. Its surface is rocky and covered with craters.

Interesting Facts

  • Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
  • It is the smallest planet in our solar system.
  • One year on Mercury lasts 88 Earth days.
  • Mercury has no moons.
  • Temperatures change greatly between day and night.

Temperature

Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold heat. This means its surface becomes extremely hot during the day and very cold at night.
