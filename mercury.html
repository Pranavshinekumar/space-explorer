PLANET 01 / SOLAR SYSTEM

Mercury

The smallest planet in our Solar System and the closest world to the Sun. Its ancient, crater-covered surface holds clues to the early history of our cosmic neighbourhood.

Mercury at a glance

Interesting Facts

  • Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
  • It is the smallest planet in our Solar System.
  • One year on Mercury lasts 88 Earth days.
  • Mercury has no natural moons.

Temperature

Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold heat. Its surface can reach around 430°C during the day and fall to around -180°C at night.
