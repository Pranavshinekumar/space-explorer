Mercury

The smallest planet in our Solar System and the closest world to the Sun. Its ancient, crater-covered surface holds clues to the early history of our cosmic neighbourhood.

Explore Mercury

🌑 A world of craters

Mercury's rocky surface is covered in impact craters, somewhat like Earth's Moon. These marks record billions of years of collisions with objects from space.

☀️ Extreme temperatures

Daytime temperatures can reach about 430°C, while nighttime temperatures can fall to around -180°C. Mercury has almost no atmosphere to retain heat.

⏳ Strange days

Mercury rotates once in about 59 Earth days. One complete sunrise-to-sunrise cycle lasts about 176 Earth days, even longer than its year!

🧲 A giant metal core

Mercury has an unusually large iron-rich core for its size. It also has a magnetic field, although it is much weaker than Earth's.

4 fascinating facts

FACT 01

The fastest planet

Mercury travels around the Sun faster than any other planet, moving at roughly 47 kilometres per second.

FACT 02

Not the hottest

Despite being closest to the Sun, Mercury is not the hottest planet. Venus is hotter because of its thick, heat-trapping atmosphere.

FACT 03

Ice near the Sun?

Water ice may exist inside permanently shadowed craters near Mercury's poles, where sunlight never reaches the crater floors.

FACT 04

Robotic explorers

NASA's MESSENGER spacecraft orbited Mercury and mapped its surface. No human has ever visited the planet.
