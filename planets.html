THE PLANETS

Explore the eight worlds of our Solar System.

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

Venus

A hot world covered by a thick atmosphere.

Earth

Our home planet and the only world known to support life.

Mars

The red planet with a cold, rocky surface.

Jupiter

The largest planet in our Solar System.

Saturn

A giant planet famous for its spectacular rings.

Uranus

An icy giant that rotates at an unusual angle.

Neptune

The distant blue planet with extremely powerful winds.
